TDMod.Net is the home of the **Tropical Diseases Modelling Network (TDModNet)**, a regional network of researchers who use mathematical and economic modelling to study the transmission, control, and elimination of tropical diseases.

The network is coordinated from the Mathematical and Economic Modelling (MAEMOD) department at the Mahidol Oxford Tropical Medicine Research Unit (MORU) in Bangkok, and works with universities, national disease control programmes, and public health agencies across Southeast Asia and the wider tropics.

Our work spans malaria elimination, antimicrobial resistance, health economics, and neglected tropical diseases such as leptospirosis, melioidosis, dengue, and tuberculosis. Alongside research, the network trains new modellers and data analysts across Southeast Asia, and publishes its models, tools, and code openly so that others can reuse and build on them.

Use this site to follow network news and training, browse modelling projects and tools, find publications, and see current opportunities to work with us.
