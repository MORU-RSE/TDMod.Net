Opportunities to join the Tropical Diseases Modelling Network, whether as a member of staff, a student, or a visiting researcher.

## What gets posted here

- **Research positions.** Modellers, epidemiologists, statisticians, health economists, data scientists, and research software engineers, based at MORU in Bangkok or with partner institutions in the region.
- **Fellowships and postdoctoral posts.** Including externally funded fellowships hosted by the network.
- **Student projects.** PhD and DPhil projects, MSc dissertation topics, and research placements. Network members supervise students at Mahidol University and the University of Oxford.
- **Internships and short attachments.** Shorter placements for students and early-career researchers wanting modelling experience.
- **Expressions of interest.** Open calls for people who want to work with the network when no specific vacancy is advertised.

## Who we are looking for

Backgrounds vary widely across the network — mathematics, statistics, epidemiology, economics, computer science, medicine, and public health are all well represented. What matters more than a specific degree is quantitative ability, comfort with data and code (R, Python, or similar), and an interest in applying modelling to real disease control problems in the region.

Positions based in Thailand are open to Thai and international applicants; several roles are also open to candidates based in partner countries.

## If nothing is currently listed

Vacancies appear here as they open. When there is nothing advertised that fits, you are still welcome to send an expression of interest describing your background and research interests, or to explore [collaboration routes]({{< relref "/collaboration" >}}).

Formally advertised MORU positions are also listed on the [MORU job opportunities page](https://www.tropmedres.ac/work-and-study/jobs), which covers both locally recruited roles and posts advertised through the University of Oxford.
