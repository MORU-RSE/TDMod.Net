Modelling projects, software tools, analysis workflows, and reproducible research resources produced by the network and its partners.

Wherever possible, models are released as open code or as interactive applications that programme staff and other researchers can run themselves, without needing to reimplement the underlying analysis.

## Project areas

- **Malaria elimination.** Transmission models calibrated to national surveillance and intervention data, used to compare elimination strategies and their cost efficiency with National Malaria Control Programmes across the Greater Mekong Subregion.
- **Antimicrobial resistance.** Models of resistance emergence and spread, hospital transmission and infection control, economic burden of resistance, and machine-learning tools for antibiotic prescribing.
- **Neglected tropical diseases.** Transmission and burden models for leptospirosis, melioidosis, dengue, tuberculosis, and hepatitis, often combined with survey and community data.
- **Health economics and implementation.** Cost-effectiveness analyses of diagnostics, treatments, and vaccines, extended to how interventions perform in routine care.
- **Methods and infrastructure.** Statistical methods, model-fitting frameworks, data repositories, and teaching tools that support the work above.

## Selected tools

A number of network models are available as interactive web applications and open repositories:

- [MAEMOD R package](https://github.com/slphyx/maemod) — a framework for building and running compartmental models, also available as a [web application](https://slphyx.shinyapps.io/maemodshiny/).
- [METCAP](https://moru.shinyapps.io/METCAP/) — Malaria Elimination Transmission and Costing in the Asia-Pacific.
- [Malaria elimination strategy tool](https://moru.shinyapps.io/MDA_eff/) — web-based modelling for *Plasmodium falciparum* elimination strategy design.
- [Cambodia Malaria Outbreak Detection Tool](https://moru.shinyapps.io/CambodiaODT/) — outbreak detection for the national programme.
- [MARC Malaria Data Repository](https://moru.shinyapps.io/myanmar_data_repository/) — malaria data repository for Myanmar.
- [Artemisinin resistance identification](https://malaria.shinyapps.io/id_artemisinin_resistance/) — identifying resistance from parasite clearance half-life data ([code](https://github.com/MAEMOD-MORU/mixmodelpaperfinal)).
- [G6PD screening cost-effectiveness](https://malaria.shinyapps.io/g6pd_screening/) — economic evaluation of screening before radical cure.
- [Economic cost of AMR](https://moru.shinyapps.io/amrcost/) and [ceftriaxone resistance prediction](https://moru.shinyapps.io/ahc-ml-amr-cost/).
- [Hepatitis E vaccination model](https://moru.shinyapps.io/HEVmodel/) and [hepatitis C screening and treatment model](https://moru.shinyapps.io/webHepC/).
- [SEGRITS](https://moru.shinyapps.io/SERIES/) — segmented regression for interrupted time series.
- [Hat Game](https://moru.shinyapps.io/hatgame/) — a simple SIR model used for teaching and public engagement ([code](https://github.com/slphyx/hatgame)).

Individual project pages in this section give the research question, model scope, team, funders, code and data availability, related publications, and current status.
