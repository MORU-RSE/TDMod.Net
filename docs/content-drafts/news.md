Updates from across the Tropical Diseases Modelling Network — what members are working on, where they are presenting, and what is open to join.

Posts in this section typically cover:

- **Training and short courses.** Infectious disease modelling courses, R and statistics training, and teaching contributions to the MSc programmes at Mahidol University and the University of Oxford.
- **Workshops and network meetings.** Regional modelling workshops, project meetings, and sessions run with national disease control programmes and ministry partners.
- **Seminars and conferences.** Talks given by network members, invited seminars, and conference sessions.
- **Field and programme activities.** Data collection, surveys, and work carried out with partners in Thailand, Cambodia, Lao PDR, Myanmar, Vietnam, and other partner settings.
- **Software and tool releases.** New models, dashboards, and analysis tools, or significant updates to existing ones.
- **Network announcements.** New members and students, awards, funding, and calls for participation.

Announcements for open positions and student projects are posted separately under [Jobs]({{< relref "/jobs" >}}). Past items remain available through the [archive]({{< relref "/archives" >}}).
