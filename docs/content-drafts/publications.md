Research outputs from members of the Tropical Diseases Modelling Network, covering malaria elimination, antimicrobial resistance, health economics, and neglected tropical diseases.

Entries in this section include peer-reviewed papers, preprints, technical reports, policy briefs, and methods notes. Where a publication has an accompanying model or tool, the record links to the code, data, and the corresponding project page under [Models / Projects]({{< relref "/models" >}}).

Each record gives citation details and a DOI or journal link, the abstract or a short plain-language summary, code and data availability, funder acknowledgements, and links to any related news posts.

Many network publications appear under the affiliations of MORU and its partner institutions. For the full institutional record, see the [MORU publications library](https://www.tropmedres.ac/publications) and the [MAEMOD department pages](https://www.tropmedres.ac/units/moru-bangkok/mathematical-and-economic-modelling).
